## Case studies

### Bounce Daily (/work/bouncedaily-redesign)
Redesigning the rider experience, from finding a scooter to staying on the road. A 2-month end-to-end redesign of the Bounce Daily rider app (scooter subscriptions for delivery and gig workers). Role: Product Designer · 2 months · end-to-end redesign.
- Framing: "A scooter isn't just a vehicle when your income depends on it." The goal was to make every critical action easier to understand, complete and recover from.
- Inherited: the product worked but had grown fragmented as flows and states were added.
- Approach: the app as a rider lifecycle: Discover → Choose → Pay → Verify → Ride → Manage → Renew. Each stage answers: Where am I? What is happening? What should I do next?
- 01 Onboarding: show the journey before asking the rider to begin it.
- 02 Hub discovery: from a list of hub names to a map-first "closest available scooter".
- 03 Choosing the ride: one decision at a time; "Don't make users remember information they can compare."
- 04 Payment: "Money-related screens should remove questions, not create them."
- Beyond the happy path: designed states for KYC (not started → uploading → submitted → approved/rejected → retry), plan lifecycle (active → expiring → expired) and kilometres (available → running low → exhausted).
- New: scooter switching (compare → check eligibility → complete requirements → find a hub → switch) and loyalty tiers (Bronze → Silver → Gold).
- System: 60+ reusable components; custom illustrations for availability, verification, service states and promotions.
- What changed: list-first → map-first; fragmented booking → guided decisions; unclear states → status + next action; reactive → proactive alerts; inconsistent patterns → design system; transaction-focused → retention and rewards.
- Outcome: a clearer core journey and better state visibility across KYC, payments, availability, kilometres and subscriptions.

### Spider-Verse cards (/work/spiderverse)
"What if the Spider-Verse chose you?" Live at spider-verse-cards.netlify.app. A side project turning the thrill of opening a trading-card pack into an interactive web experience: designed in Figma, explored with ChatGPT, vibe-coded in Cursor (Next.js, React, Framer Motion, Tailwind, Capacitor for iOS).
- Story: a childhood love of trading cards and the seconds before the reveal. ChatGPT wrote text blueprints for 7 Spider-People and card templates (it couldn't draw the copyrighted characters, so she placed the art herself).
- Build details: 5.8s orbit before the pick, 3 depth lanes, 12° tilt with a foil that follows your finger, native share sheet.
- Reception on X: 1.2K+ likes, 40K+ views, 180+ reposts, 120+ replies. X handle: @GuptaSanskritii.
