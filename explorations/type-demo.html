03 · A longer read

My mind never stops thinking. It's a boon and a pain. Most days it means I notice the small things first: a label that says too much, a button that hides, a screen that asks for patience it hasn't earned.

I like design that feels lived in, the way a well-used notebook does. Clear enough to trust, a little imperfect, and always open to a new idea.

Headings: Redaction (clean). Highlights: a worn cut of Redaction, used in place of italics, in headings and inside body text.

Body: Courier New (or Cutive Mono, a light old-typewriter font). Soft adds only a faint ink blur. Printed and Worn also roughen the letter edges. The words stay real text, so they can be selected and read by screen readers.

Sizes: headings 72 / 36, body 20 / 16 / 13.
